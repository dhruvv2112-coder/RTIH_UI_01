Run the live demo

  1. Open all three apps side by side, or the dashboard here and the two phone apps on phones (scan the codes above; same Wi-Fi).
  2. Pilgrim app: finish or skip registration, then press and hold the red SOS button for one second.
  3. Dashboard: the SOS lands at the top of the alert queue with a chime. Press Dispatch, pick Ramesh Kumar (tagged “Field app”) and send.
  4. Field app: the INCOMING DISPATCH screen appears. Hold Accept for one second. The pilgrim now sees Ramesh, his distance and ETA.
  5. Field app: add a note, then hold Complete on the task. The dashboard closes the alert and the pilgrim's SOS screen confirms help arrived.
  6. Also try: decline with a reason, recall a dispatch from the dashboard toast, file a field report, trigger the staff SOS, or activate the emergency protocol and watch it reach both phones.
